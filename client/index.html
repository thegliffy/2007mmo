offline

Walk the grass. Gather from bramble and hazel. Crush berries at the millstone, then bake or roast at the hearth. East of the hamlet, the scars hold copper and tin — pick, kiln, anvil. South of the stile, Thornkin and a Brambleback keep the briar-woods. Click near a beast to fight. WASD if you like.

Vitals

Click near a southern beast to fight. Melee trains on damage dealt, Defense on blows taken. Soft defeat wakes you at the stile.

Skills

Foraging and cooking in the hamlet, Mining and Smithing in the eastern scars, Melee and Defense in the briar-woods.

The day’s loop

  1. Pick brambleberries or hazel nuts
  2. Crush berries on the millstone into pulp
  3. Bake pulp — or roast a nut — at the hearth
  4. Walk south into the briar-woods and click near a Thornkin
  5. Click the sack it leaves behind to gather what fell
  6. Chop a tree for logs — pulp one at the millstone for paper
  7. Drop a log and right-click it to light a fire you can cook at
  8. Click Wend the Pedlar by the millstone to sell what you gather — she stocks a quarry pick
  9. Walk east into the scars, mine copper and tin, smelt a bronze bar at the kiln, forge a bronze knife at the anvil
